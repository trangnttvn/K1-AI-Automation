# Job Description: Business Partnership Manager (Fiat) - Binance

**Location:** Hanoi, Vietnam
**Department:** Business Development & Partnerships

## Role Overview
The Business Partnership Manager (Fiat) at Binance is a strategic business development position focused on expanding the company’s fiat currency infrastructure and payment capabilities within the Asia-Pacific market. The primary objective of this role is to build and manage relationships with financial institutions and payment service providers to facilitate seamless fiat-to-crypto transactions (on-ramps and off-ramps) for Binance users.

## Key Responsibilities
*   **Partnership Development:** Identify, build, and scale strategic alliances with local banks, payment service providers (PSPs), card schemes, acquirers, and fintech companies.
*   **Infrastructure Expansion:** Drive the growth of local fiat capabilities, including domestic pay-in/payout rails, bank transfers, card acquiring, and merchant settlement solutions.
*   **Full-Cycle Sales Management:** Own the entire enterprise partnership lifecycle, from market mapping and prospecting to commercial structuring, contract negotiation, and final execution.
*   **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Coordinate with internal teams—including Compliance, Legal, Risk, Product, Treasury, and Operations—to ensure partnerships are compliant and technically integrated.
*   **Operational Oversight:** Lead the end-to-end onboarding process, including KYB (Know Your Business) verification, regulatory reviews, fund flow alignment, and technical testing to ensure timely go-live.
*   **Strategy & Analysis:** Monitor industry trends, regulatory changes, and competitive landscapes to refine regional strategies and meet KPIs related to transaction volume, success rates, and coverage.

## Required Qualifications & Skills
*   **Experience:** 6–10 years of experience in payments, fintech, banking, acquiring, or large digital wallet ecosystems.
*   **Technical Knowledge:** Deep understanding of payment infrastructure, domestic rails, settlement flows, reconciliation, and risk controls.
*   **Network:** A strong existing network of relationships with banks, PSPs, and financial institutions in the target region (e.g., APAC).
*   **Soft Skills:** Proven ability to engage with C-suite and senior-level stakeholders, negotiate complex deals, and operate effectively in highly regulated environments.
*   **Language:** Fluency in English is essential; proficiency in additional regional languages is often considered a significant advantage.
